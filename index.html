CHAPTER 01 · THE BEGINNING

It started with a conversation

Every story has a beginning. Ours began with a conversation on 10 September. It was just two people getting to know each other, exchanging messages and slowly becoming a part of each other's everyday routine. Neither of us knew where those conversations would lead, or how many memories we would eventually collect along the way. Looking back, it feels special that something which became so important started with something as simple as a hello.

“We didn't know it then, but this was the beginning of us.”

CHAPTER 02 · OUR FIRST MEETING

Finally, in the same place

After days of conversations, we finally got to meet in person. V3S became the setting for our first meeting. I remember waiting outside the metro, figuring out where to meet you, and then finally seeing you. We spent the afternoon talking, laughing, flirting and walking around. We shared parts of our lives, spoke about our pasts and the things that had shaped us. There were little moments too: sharing songs and earbuds, having chai together, and simply enjoying being around each other. Somewhere between all those conversations, I told you that I liked you. It was our first meeting, but it already felt like the beginning of something worth remembering.

“A place became special because it was where I first met you.”

CHAPTER 03 · OUR FIRST KISS

A date where the movie was secondary

We went to the cinema, but the movie probably wasn't the most memorable part of that day. We spent so much time talking about ourselves, sharing thoughts and getting closer that the screen became almost secondary. And then came our first kiss. It was one of those moments that doesn't need a long explanation. A memory that stays with you because of how it felt, not because of how much happened around it.

“We went to watch a movie, but I found a memory I never want to forget.”

CHAPTER 04 · A DAY AT HOME

A little closer

You came over, and we got to spend the entire day together. There was no big plan or special destination to reach. We simply had time with each other. Sometimes the most meaningful memories aren't made while travelling somewhere or doing something extraordinary. They're made in ordinary spaces, when you feel comfortable enough to just be yourself. That day became one of those memories for me. A reminder that being together can be special even when the world outside carries on as usual.

“It wasn't about where we were. It was about having you there.”

CHAPTER 05 · RAJIV CHOWK

Our Rajiv Chowk moments

Some places become special not because of what they have, but because of the people and memories we leave behind there. Rajiv Chowk slowly became one of those places for us. What might have started as just another place to meet turned into our own little corner of the city, a place where we began creating memories that would become a part of our story. For a little while, Rajiv Chowk became our go-to place. The random plans, the unexpected meetings, the time we spent together, and all those little moments made it feel familiar in a way that only a special place can. We didn't always need a big plan or a special reason to be there. Sometimes, just meeting each other was enough to make the day memorable. It became a place where our story grew through ordinary days and spontaneous moments. And the best part is that this isn't a chapter we're closing. It's a place we'll keep returning to, with more memories waiting to be made, more stories to collect, and more moments that will always belong to us.

“Some places become ours without us ever having to claim them. Rajiv Chowk will always be one of those places.”

CHAPTER 06 · DEER PARK

Finding our way back

Not every chapter is easy. We had a difficult patch, and for a moment, things between us felt uncertain. There were emotions, misunderstandings and things that needed to be said. But on 2 October, we met again at Deer Park. We sat together, talked things through and tried to understand each other. We chose to work through what had happened instead of letting that difficult moment define our story. And we kissed. I remember that moment as something deeper than just a kiss. It felt like finding our way back to each other.

“Some memories don't need photographs. I remember that day by heart.”

CHAPTER 07 · The Hardest Weeks

Weeks of Struggle

Not every part of our story was easy. The weeks that followed brought some of the hardest moments we had to face together. There were misunderstandings, disagreements, moments of doubt, and times when the distance between us felt bigger than it really was. Sometimes, things were left unsaid. Sometimes, we struggled to understand what the other person was feeling. There were days when emotions took over, when little things turned into bigger conversations, and when we both had to figure out how to handle situations we had never faced together before. We weren't always able to understand each other perfectly. We made mistakes, got frustrated, and had moments when everything felt uncertain. But somewhere in all of that, we were also learning. Learning that loving someone isn't just about enjoying the beautiful days. It's also about listening when things get difficult, being patient when emotions run high, understanding each other's silences, and making an effort even when finding the right words feels impossible. We had to learn that we couldn't always expect each other to understand everything without explaining it. That sometimes, reassurance matters, sometimes space is needed, and sometimes all a person wants is to feel heard and understood. Those weeks weren't our favourite memories, but they became an important part of our story. They showed us that we were still figuring each other out, still growing, and still learning how to take care of what we were building together. We didn't have all the answers, and we certainly didn't get everything right. But every conversation, every attempt to fix things, and every time we chose to understand each other taught us something about us.

“We weren't perfect, and neither were our days. But even through the hardest ones, you were someone I still wanted to understand.”

CHAPTER 08 · SANJAY VAN

A day in the trees

Sanjay Van gave us an entire day to remember. We reached the forest, shared kisses and little moments, walked around and spent time near the waterfall. We had our sutta, ate together, talked, laughed and explored. We trekked and eventually found a spot overlooking the forest. We sat on that cliff for almost two hours, taking in the view and enjoying the time together. We made photographs and videos, but some of the best parts of the day were simply the conversations and the quiet moments. The journey back was its own memory. After all the walking and exploring, you fell asleep in my arms during the ride. It was peaceful, and I found myself resting too. From the waterfall to the cliff, from the little adventures to that quiet ride home, the day gave us so many moments to keep.

“The forest was beautiful, but my favourite view was you beside me.”

CHAPTER 09 · Our Little Moments

Times When We Were Together

Not every memory was planned. Sometimes, I would randomly come to meet you, just because I wanted to see you. Sometimes, a normal day would turn into an unexpected little adventure because we decided to spend some time together. Those random meetings slowly became one of my favourite parts of us. There were surprise moments at V3S, unexpected plans around Rajiv Chowk, and those times when neither of us really needed a reason to meet. Even if it was only for a little while, seeing you made an ordinary day feel different. A few conversations, silly jokes, stolen moments together, and suddenly we had another memory to keep. And then there were our absolutely crazy tales. The drunk adventures, the unplanned decisions, the random conversations, the uncontrollable laughter, and the stories that sound even funnier every time we remember them. Some nights were chaotic, some moments were completely ridiculous, but they became stories that only we could understand. Between the surprise visits, the spontaneous meet-ups, our little adventures, and the quiet moments when it was just us talking, we kept collecting memories without even realising it. Not every moment needed a photograph or a special occasion. Sometimes, all it took was showing up and being together.

“The best memories weren't always planned. Sometimes, they were just me finding another reason to see you.”

CHAPTER 10 · TWO MONTHS

And the pages keep turning

Two months ago, we were just two people starting a conversation. Since then, we've met, laughed, explored places, shared our first kiss, made memories and learned more about each other. Our story hasn't been made up of perfect days. It has had its difficult moments too. But every chapter, whether easy or difficult, has become a part of the journey that brought us here. When I look at this little map, I don't just see locations. I see the first time we met, the places we visited, the conversations we shared and all the little moments that made these two months ours. This is only a small collection of everything we've experienced. There are still so many places to visit, photographs to take and memories waiting to be made.

“Two months down. So many little moments, and so much more of our map left to draw.”

10 NOVEMBER 2026

To my Soumya,

Two months might sound like a small amount of time, but look at all the little worlds we've made inside it. Think about how a simple conversation turned into countless conversations, how two people who were once strangers slowly became a part of each other's days, and how ordinary places in Delhi started holding memories that neither of us could have planned.

When I look back at 10 September, I smile knowing that neither of us had any idea what was waiting ahead. We didn't know about our first meeting, our first kiss, our random plans, our unexpected surprises, our long conversations, or all the little adventures that would eventually become our story. We were just getting to know each other, one conversation at a time.

And then came all those places. V3S, where I first got to meet you. The cinema, where we made a memory that had very little to do with the movie. Deer Park, where we found our way through a difficult moment. Rajiv Chowk, which became our little go-to place for a while. V3S, with its unexpected meetings and surprises. Sanjay Van, with its forest paths, waterfall, cliffside conversations and that peaceful journey back.

There are places in this city that will never feel quite ordinary to me again. A corner, a path, a quiet view, a metro station, a familiar meeting spot, a ride home. They carry pieces of our story now. I know that whenever I pass through some of these places, a little part of me will remember the time we spent there.

I love that not all our memories were carefully planned. Some of my favourite moments came from random decisions, spontaneous meetings and those times when I simply wanted to come and see you. The little surprises, the silly jokes, the unexpected adventures, and even our crazy drunk stories have become a part of the collection of memories that belongs only to us.

I also know that our story hasn't always been easy. We've had misunderstandings, difficult conversations, moments of frustration, doubts and days when understanding each other wasn't simple. There were times when we both struggled with our emotions and didn't know exactly what to say or how to make things better.

But I don't want to pretend that those moments never happened. They're a part of our story too. They remind me that we're still learning each other, still discovering how to communicate, and still figuring out how to be there for one another. I don't expect every page to be perfect. I just hope we keep being honest, patient, kind, and willing to understand.

I hope you always feel comfortable being yourself around me. I hope you never feel that you have to hide your thoughts or feelings just to keep things peaceful. I want us to be able to talk, laugh, disagree, explain, listen and understand without forgetting that we're on the same side. I want to keep learning the little things about you, even the ones you think nobody notices.

Thank you for the laughter, the conversations, the random meetings, the surprises, the photographs, the kisses, the adventures and all the moments in between. Thank you for making ordinary days memorable and for becoming a part of so many little things in my life. Even the simplest moments have found a place in this map because they happened with you.

I don't know exactly what every future chapter will look like. I don't want to make promises just because they sound beautiful. What I do know is that I want to keep showing up, keep making memories, keep understanding you better, and keep giving this story the care it deserves.

There are still so many places we haven't visited, so many photographs we haven't taken, so many conversations we haven't had, and so many little adventures waiting for us. This map is only a beginning. I hope we keep adding pins to it, one memory at a time.

And someday, when we look back at this little website, I hope we can smile at how much life we managed to fit into these first two months.

Happy two months, Soumya. Thank you for being a part of my story. I'm glad that, among all the people and places in this world, our paths crossed.

Wherever the map takes us, I'd still choose the place where you are. And if we ever get lost along the way, I hope we remember that the best part of this journey was never the destination. It was the little moments we shared getting there.

“This map holds our places, our memories, and little pieces of us. But my favourite part of the whole story will always be you.”

Yours, Pranav Pathak ♡
